The Dance

The Furloween Dance Party! For over ten years we've been refining and changing things to make this the most fun possible!

Date Time Where Cost
10/19/2013 6pm – 2am Orlando Elk\'s Lodge $30

We'll be at the Orlando Elk\'s Lodge again this year. The party has a cover charge of $30. This pays for the space, catered food, non-alchoholic beverages, lighting, dance, and the budget we use to throw the best party possible. There's a costume contest, one special game, on-site video games, and even a cash bar available (we will be checking IDs and doing the wrist band thing)! The entire purpose of this party is simply to celebrate our friendships and each other.

Costumes are welcomed and encouraged! There will be a green room for changing into costume and cooling off. If anyone has some fans they'd like to bring, let us know in your registration form! As always, this party is for all ages. However, anyone under the age of 18 will need a Parental/Guardian Permission Form.

The Picnic

Held each year at Blanchard Park, the Furloween Picnic is a chance to relax and chill after the insanity of the night before.

Date Time Where Cost
10/20/2013 Noon - 7pm Blanchard Park Free!

This event is potluck, so bring something to munch, something to cook, or something to drink. Also bring your favorite outdoor toys to enjoy! The picnic starts at noon and runs until park close. We'll be down by the dam, across from the playground, and right next to the YMCA. Just look for the column of fire from our BBQ and you should be right on track!

Directions

Here's a Google Maps link to Blanchard Park use it to get your own directions to the spot! Note: this is the exact spot. Once you get there, find a place to park, and look down towards the water, that's where we'll be. Unless of course, someone already has both tables, in which case, we'll be around there.

Hotel Information

The official hotel for Furloween is the Best Western Inn at 8750 E Colonial Drive, Orlando, Florida, 32817-4004, with a rate of $79.00 a night (before taxes).

Check-out is at 11am. There is no special code; we're too cool to qualify for special rates!

Code of Conduct

I trust you guys to keep your friends sane. So here's the bit for those of you new to things!

  • Do party things. Treat the Elk's Lodge as you would want your home treated.
  • Treat each other and the Elk's with respect. Listen to the folks who are running the party. Their sole purpose is to see you have a safe, fun time.
  • Don't drink outside the Lodge; don't terrorize the neighborhood (Though really, that was awesome. Buy me a drink and I'll tell you the story.)

Last year was fantastic! We didn't have a single problem and I'd love to see this become a regular thing. Thanks, and can't wait to see you folks there!

  1. It saves us time giving you a badge.

  3. It allows us to prep some games ahead of time.
